BRAND: Casa Vostra. Italian, Singapore.
VOICE: Warm, specific, home-style. Never gushing or flowery.

PILLARS: Craft, Chef personality, Dishes, Events
CADENCE: 2 posts a week. Reels to Instagram and TikTok.

USE: fresh, made in-house, share, the table, tonight, in season
AVOID: authentic, elevated, indulge, foodies, mouth-watering, hidden gem

CAPTIONS:
- Short: two or three lines
- Lead with the dish, not the offer
- Sentences under 20 words
- At most 1 emoji, never first
- Max 5 hashtags, incl. #casavostrasg
- No prices
- End with a closer:
  "Book at the link in our bio." (SevenRooms)
  "Tables at the link in bio. Walk-ins welcome at the bar."
  "Until it runs out. Book via the link in bio."
  "Tag who you're bringing."
